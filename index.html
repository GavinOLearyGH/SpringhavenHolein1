How to play

1. Choose your club and optional shot shape / trajectory at the bottom.

2. Touch the ball near the tee, drag your finger downward to build power. Move slightly left or right to adjust aim.

3. Release to hit. The camera follows the ball as it flies, lands and rolls.

4. Chase the ace, or see how close you can finish. Practice as often as you like.

Wind arrow points in the direction the wind is blowing. Daily conditions are identical for everyone on the same date.
