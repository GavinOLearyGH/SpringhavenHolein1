How to play

Touch the highlighted ball at the bottom of the course. Pull downward and sideways like a slingshot. The farther you pull, the more power. Release to launch.

Drag elsewhere on the course to look left or right. This is a mobile 3D prototype; pinch zoom and detailed terrain are future refinements.

The wind arrow shows the direction the wind is blowing. Flight is simulated with drag, lift, gravity and wind-relative velocity.
